Rules

  • Weeks 1–2: 3–4 reps in reserve.
  • Weeks 3–4: 2–3 RIR.
  • Weeks 5–8: mostly 1–2 RIR.
  • Weeks 9–11: strength/hypertrophy peak.
  • Week 12: deload + optional rep/strength checks.

Progression

When every set reaches the top of its rep range with the target RIR, add the smallest practical load next time. Upper body: usually +1–2.5 kg. Lower body: usually +2.5–5 kg.
